Hydrogen plant by steam methane reforming

A plant that turns natural gas and steam into about 213 tonnes of high-purity hydrogen a day. I'm building it in Aspen Plus, from reaction kinetics and heat integration through equipment sizing and costing.

What I'm doing

  • Built the Aspen Plus flowsheet with kinetics from the literature for the reformer and the high- and low-temperature shift reactors.
  • Ran a pinch analysis in Aspen Energy Analyzer to set utility targets and guide heat recovery.
  • Sized the reactors, fired heaters, heat exchangers and PSA beds, and costed the plant in Aspen Process Economic Analyzer.
  • Oversaw a teammate's price comparison of Thai natural-gas sources (Gulf, Myanmar, LNG and pool gas) for the feed.

How the plant works

The feed is cleaned, reformed with steam at 900 °C, then shifted to turn carbon monoxide into more hydrogen. The hot gas raises steam at three pressure levels on its way down to 40 °C, and a pressure-swing adsorption unit separates the hydrogen. The two shift reactors cut carbon monoxide from 27.0 to 2.5 t/h.
